## App name

Ryser — Adaptive Alarm

## Short description

Local alarm clock with wake missions, Wake Checks, and explainable adaptation.

## Full description

Ryser is an Android alarm clock for people who want more than a dismiss button.
Create alarms, choose a sound, and add a walking, light-jump, or registered QR
mission. Optional Wake Checks ask you to confirm that you are still awake after
the mission.

Adaptive Wake uses recorded alarm interactions to prepare future settings. It
looks at response time, snoozes, mission results, and Wake Checks. Changes have
an explanation, follow your selected limits, and can be paused. You can keep
individual settings fixed, set a maximum playback level, or reset learning
while keeping your wake history.

Features include recurring alarms, configurable snooze, increasing playback
volume, vibration patterns, bundled sounds, local wake history, protocol
explanations, and a weekly summary. Alarm Health helps you review Android access
needed for reliable scheduling and presentation.

Alarm execution and history are designed to work locally. The release uses a
bundled QR model and does not require a Ryser account. Optional camera/activity/
foreground-location access supports the chosen mission. Android alarm volume,
permissions, device settings, and manufacturer power management affect behavior.

Use walking and jumping missions only where it is safe and appropriate. Ryser
does not provide medical sleep analysis or guarantee waking.

## Reviewer instructions

No account or paid access is required. Grant notifications and Alarms & reminders;
review full-screen alarm access in Alarm Health. Schedule an alarm two minutes
ahead and lock the phone. Test a simple dismiss first. For QR, register a QR code
you control before selecting that mission. For movement/jumping, use a safe area.
Enable Wake Check for post-mission confirmation; review the configured delay.
Emergency Stop requires an eight-second hold. Adaptive behavior requires real,
completed comparable wake evidence; do not expect an immediate first-alarm change.
